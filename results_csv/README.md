# PRAM result tables (CSV)

Result files of the PRAM analysis: Supplementary Material (17 tables and 4 figures) and the main text. The analysis code is in the `code/` and `notebooks/` folders of this repository. These files hold the result grids that the tables of the Supplementary Material and of the main text do not print. `table_map.csv` lists, for every table and figure, the files that hold its values, in the words of the supplement guide.

Every row is an aggregate: a strategy in a bank draw, a hospital, or a summary of them. No file has one row per patient, and the three source databases (MIMIC-III, MIMIC-IV, eICU-CRD) are not redistributed.

## Folders

| Folder | Files | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| (this folder) | 75 | result grids of the notebooks |
| `derived/` | 7 | counts and hospital-level statistics that the analysis scripts computed from the files of this folder (Table S4 Panels B and C, the intervals of Table S8 Panel A, Table S11 Panel C, main Table 5 Panel A) |
| `legacy/` | 62 | one file per table panel, with the cell text unchanged |

## Hospital labels, suppressed cells and rounding

- The column `site` in `hospital_results_F42.csv`, `hospital_results_F33.csv`, `A6_variance_diagnosis_by_hospital.csv`, `A7_network_F42.csv` and `A7_network_F33.csv` is a label (H001 to H067) of an eICU-CRD hospital within this folder; the five files share the labels. The labels are arbitrary and do not follow the hospital numbers of eICU-CRD. A rerun of the notebooks writes its own labels, so the same hospitals can receive other labels. The files give the number of stays and events of each hospital, which the analysis needs for the weights.
- `A1_mimic4_period_counts.csv`: the event counts of the rows `not assigned` are given as `suppressed` for both period rules and both outcomes. One rule that does not depend on the values suppresses a cell with 1 to 4 events, and the four `not assigned` cells are suppressed together because the two period rules cover the same stays. In `R6_N1_hospital_alpha_distribution.csv` the count of hospital folds is given as a share with three decimals, which is the precision that Supplementary Table S14 prints; a share that corresponds to a count below 5 is shown as `<0.03`.
- `R6_N1_hospital_criteria.csv`: the share of hospitals whose weight was 0 in all three folds is given as a band (`a0_in_all_folds_hospital_band`).
- The `R7_` files hold the analysis of weights up to 1.00 and of a weight fixed at 0.50. No file has a hospital label. Counts of hospital folds, of hospitals and of draws are given as counts and are not suppressed; `n` in `R7_N1_whole_arms.csv` and `R7_N1_whole_pairdiff.csv` is the number of ICU stays of the whole eICU-CRD pool. Arm codes: `A50` and `A100` select the weight by AUROC on the grid 0 to 0.50 and 0 to 1.00, `L50` and `L100` select it by log loss, `F50` fixes it at 0.50.
- The `R8_` files hold the federated analyses. `n_hospitals` is a number of hospitals and is not suppressed; no file has a hospital label or a patient count. `mode = orig` is the original split (one random split per hospital, test 30 percent) and `mode = cv3` the three-fold split of the hospital-level analysis. In `R8_split_frame_effect.csv` the column `repro_ok` tells whether the original-split value of the row matched the reference value within the preset tolerances; for rows with `repro_ok = False` the three-fold value is shown for completeness.
- The hospital bootstrap uses 5,000 resamples with seed 20261003. Other files give the number of resamples in the column `n_boot`.
- `A6_variance_diagnosis_by_hospital.csv` (Supplementary Table S8 Panel F) holds the within-hospital bootstrap variance from 5,000 resamples per hospital (`n_boot_used` and `n_boot` are both 5000, seed in `boot_seed`), the same number of resamples as the other bootstrap intervals.

## How to read the names

- `F42` is the full set of 42 model inputs. `F33` removes the nine inputs with more than 30 percent missing values in at least one database.
- Outcomes: `AKI_168h` (acute kidney injury within 168 hours of the prediction time) and `mortality_168h` (in-hospital death within 168 hours).
- Cohorts (`target`): `MIMIC-IV` (development cohort, internal test split), `MIMIC-III`, `eICU`.
- Bank size (`bank`): 500, 1000, 2000, 5000, `full` (all local patients outside the test fold), `max` (largest bank available in the temporal analysis).
- Strategies (`method`): `b1_base` frozen logistic, `b5_base` frozen CatBoost, `pram_b1` and `pram_b5` PRAM on each, `recal_iso` isotonic recalibration, `platt_b1` and `platt_b5` logistic recalibration (Platt), `local_lr`, `local_cb` and `pooled_lr` retraining, `anchored_ridge`, `upd1_b1`, `upd2_b1`, `upd3_b1` the three updating steps, `ct_pub` closed testing as published, `ct_b1` closed testing in our variant, `pram_platt_b1` and `pram_platt_b5` PRAM followed by recalibration.
- `nb@0.06` is net benefit at a threshold of 6 percent. `nb_site` is net benefit at the representative threshold (6 percent for AKI, 20 percent for mortality), and `nb_20` at 20 percent. `ece` uses ten equal-width bins. `slope` and `intercept` are the calibration slope and intercept.
- Constants set after the results were seen: non-inferiority limit 0.01 AUROC, net benefit limit 0.001, representative thresholds, threshold ranges 2 to 10 percent (AKI) and 10 to 30 percent (mortality).
- `iw` is importance weighting (`iwc` the weights with the odds rescaled by the ratio of sample sizes, `iwn` the rescaled weights normalised to mean 1), `coral` is correlation alignment, `ar_` is anchor regression with its grouping (`2db` two databases, `period` admission periods), form (`lpm`, `probit`) and selection (`sel` by cross-validated AUC, `nll` by log loss).
- `_orig` in `A6_meta_reml_hk.csv` and `hospital_summary.csv` marks the original implementation of the paired DeLong variance, kept for the comparison in Supplementary Table S8 Panel F. Every interval and test in the paper uses the standard implementation.
- `var_share_k_` and `pub_share_k_` in the `CTPUB_` files are the shares of banks in which closed testing in our variant and as published chose step k (0 no update, 1 intercept, 2 recalibration, 3 revision).
